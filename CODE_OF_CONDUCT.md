# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, as its
code of conduct. The full text is published at:

https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

A short summary follows; the linked document is authoritative.

## Our pledge

We want SHARDCORE to be a welcoming technical community. Contributors,
maintainers, and users commit to participating in a way that is
respectful, professional, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind. Assume good faith. Critique ideas, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it constructively.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels, especially from
  people newer to the project or to the field.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior is defined by the Contributor Covenant v2.1
linked above. In short: conduct that a reasonable person would
consider harassing, discriminatory, or otherwise unwelcoming is not
allowed in this project's spaces.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces (the repository,
issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any official channels
such as chat, email, and events) and to public spaces when an individual is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Reports of conduct concerns should be sent to **conduct@shardcore.dev**.
Reports will be reviewed promptly and in confidence. The maintainers
will follow the Community Impact Guidelines from the Contributor
Covenant v2.1 when determining consequences, which range from a
private warning to a permanent ban depending on severity and pattern.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good
faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by
other maintainers.
